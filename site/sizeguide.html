Size guide

Every Dysobay piece is cut to a single size — the one listed on its product page. Use this table to convert it to your usual sizing.

How to measure

Measure over the underwear you would normally wear, keeping the tape level and snug but not tight. Bust at the fullest point, waist at the narrowest, hip 20 cm below the waist.

Between two sizes? Write to us before ordering. Because a piece is retired once it sells, we would rather answer a fit question than take one back.
