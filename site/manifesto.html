Manifesto

Seven clauses, one for each letter of the name. The number beside a letter is its position in the alphabet — D is the fourth, Y the twenty-fifth — which is where 4.25.19.15.2.1.25 comes from. A barcode is what mass production puts on a garment; ours spells the label and argues against making one.

The barcode

Four foundations. Twenty-five against fifteen minutes. Nineteen out of twenty. Return to why. The binary choice. The singular truth. And Y, the letter that splits in two.
